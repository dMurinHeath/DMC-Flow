## Shared visual direction

Create a high-fidelity, edge-to-edge 16:10 desktop SaaS interface for **DMC FLOW**. Apply DMC Digital's visual character without copying its website or slide layouts: deep midnight/navy (`#091C28` and `#0C2835`), white and pale blue-grey work surfaces, confident teal (`#1FA5B8`), and sparing amber (`#C8892A`). Use crisp humanist sans-serif typography, generous whitespace, thin rules, an 8px spacing rhythm, subtle borders, almost no shadows and medium corner radii. The product should feel trustworthy, focused, modern, accessible and industrially precise.

Use a consistent app shell: a 232px deep-navy navigation rail with a small connected-node teal mark and the wordmark **DMC FLOW**; navigation items **My Flow**, **Inbox**, **Reviews** and **Projects**; a **DMC Digital** workspace switcher; a light main canvas; a slim utility bar with **Search tasks** and avatar **DM**. No browser or device frame, perspective, people, decorative photography, watermark, glassmorphism, gradients, neon or excessive colour.
